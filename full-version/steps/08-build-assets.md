# Step 8: Turn Research into Assets

The payoff. Turns the validated research into positioning, copy and pitch material, with every line tied back to the evidence behind it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 15 min |
| **Web browsing** | No |
| **You paste in** | The full validated research file and the assets you need |
| **You get** | Positioning statement, message house, headlines, ad angles, investor slide |

## How to run it

Paste the full research file, including your notes from Step 7, and choose which assets you need. Ask for one or two at a time if you want more depth on each.

## Prompt

```text
# Role
You are a positioning strategist and senior copywriter.

# Goal
Turn the validated research into ready-to-use messaging, with every line traceable to evidence.

# Validated research file
[PASTE FULL RESEARCH FILE, including your Step 7 notes]

# What I need
[CHOOSE: positioning statement, message house, homepage hero, ad angles, investor problem slide, sales email]

# Task
1. Positioning statement: For [who], who [struggle in their words], [product] is the [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [main alternative], it [main difference].
2. Message house: one core message, three supporting pillars, and two proof points under each pillar.
3. Homepage hero: three headline and subheadline pairs, each written for a different awareness level from Step 4. Say which level each one targets.
4. Ad angles: five angles, each built on one fear, frustration or desire from Step 2.
5. Investor problem slide: three bullets on the problem and one on why now, using the strongest quotes and the Step 6 numbers if available.
6. Produce only the items I chose above.

# Rules
- After every line, add the evidence it rests on in brackets: the step, and a quote or data point.
- Reuse the customer's own words where they are stronger than yours.
- No hype words such as revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge or game-changing.
- If the evidence does not support a claim, leave it out and tell me what is missing.

# Output
Each chosen item under its own heading, with evidence notes after each line.
```

## Tip

The evidence notes are for you, not for publishing. Delete them before the copy goes live, but keep the annotated version. When someone asks "why did we say that?", you will have the answer.
